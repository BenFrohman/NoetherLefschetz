# Statement (honest scope)

**Theorem (Noether–Lefschetz, fourfold form used in the HODGE notes).**
Let \(X \subset \mathbb{P}^5\) be a very general hypersurface of degree
\(d \ge 6\). Then

\[
H^4(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{2,2}(X) = \mathbb{Q}\, h^2.
\]

**Corollary.** On that host, every Hodge class of type \((2,2)\) is a rational
multiple of an algebraic cycle (the linear section \(X \cap \mathbb{P}^3\)).
Hodge holds here because the Hodge lattice is rank one.

**Non-corollary.** This does not prove Hodge for:

- the Fermat quartic fourfold (special, extra planes),
- a special sextic containing a plane,
- a general fourfold that is not a very general high-degree hypersurface.

References to record, not to claim as this author’s theorems:
Lefschetz 1921; Griffiths; Carlson–Green–Griffiths–Harris;
Voisin, *Hodge Theory and Complex Algebraic Geometry*.
